Station dashboard

Walk with Z and S (hold Shift to run), turn with Q and D (W A S D on a QWERTY keyboard) or the arrows, jump with Space. Drag the view to look around, press V to change the view. Press E at the base's airlock door to go in or out, and at the terminal in the middle of the base to open the station dashboard, where you can take over the excavator robot: Space digs in the grey zone and unloads in the orange drop-off zone by the workshops; driving, digging and unloading drain its battery, and it stops for good when the battery is empty.
