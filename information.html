Context

Astronauts are getting ready to go back to the Moon with NASA's Artemis program, and one day to travel to Mars. In Project Arcadia, you run a small base far from Earth and learn the real choices engineers have to make.

  1. Act I Earth Getting ready for the mission
  2. Act II Moon Building the base and finding resources
  3. Act III Mars Living very far from Earth

Goal

Keep your crew safe and your base running. Nothing is free in space: saving power today can leave you short of oxygen tomorrow. Think ahead, and if something goes wrong, find out why and try again.

How to play

  1. Pick an act. The Moon is open as a demo.
  2. Read the daily briefing: it gives you the day's goals and how much of each resource you have.
  3. Launch the mission and walk around the base to reach your goals.
  4. Watch your resources: oxygen to breathe, power from the Sun, and water.
